القبول والتسجيل

Plan your admission step by step — confirm eligibility, choose a program, review your financial plan, and apply.

Step 1 — Eligibility

بكالوريوس admission requirements

  1. General Secondary Education certificate (Tawjihi) or its equivalent, with a minimum average of 60% for all majors, except Law (65%) and Pharmacy & Engineering (80%).
  2. Non-Jordanian students may be admitted up to 10 marks below the Jordanian average, following the GSE streams that govern each major.
  3. Community College Diploma holders who pass the Qualifying Exam (minimum 68%; Pharmacy & Engineering 70%) are accepted into equivalent approved majors.
  4. Holders of 3- and 4-year Diplomas may enrol through the Bridging System after passing Al-Balqa’s Qualifying Exam at the same thresholds.

Up to 50% of passed courses are credited under the Bridging System.

Documents required

  1. Original or certified Tawjihi transcript.
  2. Equivalence for foreign certificates from the Ministry of Education.
  3. Copy of passport or national ID, and birth certificate.
  4. Military service booklet (male Jordanians).
  5. Two recent photographs, 6×4 cm.

Post-graduate (Master’s) admission is based on a recognised bachelor’s degree that meets the faculty’s minimum standing, together with an English-language test score.

  • A recognised bachelor’s degree from an accredited university.
  • English proficiency — TOEFL, IELTS or the National English Exam. Without a score, a 6-credit remedial course is taken in the first year.
  • Applicants ranked below “Good” may be admitted conditionally on passing three faculty-set courses (minimum 70% each) in the first semester.

Merit scholarships

Scholarships by program.

MEU offers incentive grants to new students, scaled by academic average. The higher your average, the larger the scholarship — with a full 100% scholarship for the very top band. Grants apply to all approved study-plan hours.

The Admission & Registration Department.

The Admission and Registration Department is vital to MEU’s success, as it is one of the only departments at the University that directly connects to students and faculty members at MEU and to the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research. The Department is responsible for outlining MEU’s admission policies, helping students with the major-changing process, and running all student registration procedures.

The Department is also in charge of tracking students’ academic affairs from the point of their admission until the day of their graduation, and enforcing the rules of the Certificates and Academic Degrees Granting Guidelines. As the Department is essential to so many University processes, it must be maintained so that it can continue to efficiently serve students during their journeys to academic and professional success.
