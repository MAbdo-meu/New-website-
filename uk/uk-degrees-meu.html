A pathway that exists nowhere else in Jordan

From a diploma to a doctorate — one route

Pearson BTEC Level 5 holders can convert a diploma into a full UK bachelor's in as little as one year, then continue all the way to a UK PhD, entirely at MEU.

01

BTEC Level 5

Arrive with a Pearson Level 5 diploma in a computing or related field.

→
02

UK Bachelor's

Top up to a full UK BSc (Hons) in about one year, subject to equivalency review.

→
03

UK Master's

Progress into a UK MBA, MSc or MSc Applied Translation.

→
04

UK PhD

Continue to a UK doctorate in Amman across business, health, media and computing.

*Level 6 entry depends on a successful equivalency review of the BTEC Level 5 qualification, as determined by the University of Bedfordshire's admissions team.
