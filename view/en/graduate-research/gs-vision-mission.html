Vision & Mission

Vision

To be distinctive and to advance scientific research and postgraduate programmes.

Mission

To play a vital role in advancing postgraduate programmes and scientific research, elevating the University's standing.

Objectives

1

Enhance the culture of scientific research among faculty members.

2

Motivate faculty members and students to conduct research projects.

3

Follow the latest scientific developments in postpost-graduate studies.

4

Raise the level of scientific research by reinforcing applied research.

5

Conduct research that provides the University and local community with the best services.

6

Form active partnerships with other universities and international research centres.

7

Develop financial resources by adopting innovative research.

8

Uphold scientific integrity for researchers and students.
