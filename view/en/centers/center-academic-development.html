Overview

The Academic Development Unit organises the University's academic development within a single institutional framework, supporting faculties to continuously improve teaching, learning, and programme quality.

What the Center offers

Faculty development

Periodic workshops that build faculty academic and technical capabilities, based on training-needs analysis.

Programme & curriculum development

Support for developing and updating academic plans and programmes against local and international standards.

Academic audit

Internal audit of academic practices within the teaching and learning process.

Field training & industry projects

Coordination of practical field training and industry partnerships across specialisations.

Engaging the University community

The Unit supports colleges and departments rather than students directly — strengthening the academic core of the University. By improving how MEU teaches and assesses, it raises the quality of the education every student receives and the readiness of every graduate.

From MEU

The Academic Development Unit was established as a strategic response to the need to organise academic development within a unified institutional framework, aligning with the University's vision to enhance the quality of education, improve the efficiency of academic programmes, and ensure their compliance with local and international standards. Its creation was approved by Deans' Council Decision No. (410, 2024/2025) on 12/11/2024.

The Unit supports colleges and academic departments in improving teaching and learning practices, developing programmes and curricula, and refining faculty capabilities. It serves as a pivotal body for sustainable assessment and improvement, providing data-driven recommendations that strengthen the University's overall performance.
