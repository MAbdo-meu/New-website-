Objectives

  1. Promote commitment to continuous improvement and effective development of institutional performance across academic and administrative units.
  2. Propose policies and operational plans to ensure efficiency and high effectiveness in quality management.
  3. Support academic and administrative units in obtaining the quality assurance certificate, international academic accreditation, and national and international classifications.
  4. Provide the data and information needed to support planning and development for the University and the local community.
