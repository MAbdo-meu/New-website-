How to partner

  1. Tell us the need. Share the skills gap, audience size and preferred delivery window.
  2. Co-design the programme. A faculty lead scopes content, contact hours and assessment.
  3. Agree the terms. A training agreement is signed and registered with the university.
  4. Deliver and certify. Training runs on campus or at your site, with MEU certification on completion.
