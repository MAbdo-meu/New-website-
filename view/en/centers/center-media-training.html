Overview

The Media Training and Development Center is a working production facility inside the Faculty of Media, giving students professional studios, equipment, and hands-on training in modern audio and video production.

What the Center offers

Studio & production facilities

A TV studio, two radio studios, editing rooms, and labs equipped with industry-standard media production software.

Vocational media training

Courses in news broadcast, radio and TV production and presentation, direction, filming, montage, and on-air presence.

Cross-faculty access

Open enrolment for students from any faculty who want to build communication and media-production skills.

Industry-aligned skills

Training designed around current industry techniques and ethical and legal standards in media practice.

Engaging the University community

The Center gives students real production experience before they graduate, supports faculty teaching with professional infrastructure, and welcomes learners from across the University — making media skills a shared resource rather than a single-faculty asset.

From MEU

The Media Training and Development Center was founded as part of MEU's Faculty of Media to realise the University's strategy of enhancing the quality of education MEU provides its students. It is dedicated to teaching students how to produce high-quality audio and video products using the industry's latest multimedia techniques.

With its TV studio, two radio studios, editing rooms, and computer labs equipped with the industry's most advanced media production software, the Center offers training courses in a variety of vocational and communication skills — including news broadcast, radio, TV programme production and presentation, field investigation, radio and TV direction, filming and montage, on-air body language, and audio and video production techniques. The Center welcomes students from other faculties interested in advancing their communication and media production skills.
