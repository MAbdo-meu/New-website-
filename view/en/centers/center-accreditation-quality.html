Overview

The Accreditation, Quality & Information Center safeguards the standards that underpin every MEU degree. As an independent entity, it drives quality assurance, national and international accreditation, and the University's institutional data base.

What the Center offers

Quality assurance

Implementation and monitoring of the quality management system, including ISO 9001:2015 compliance and the eight national quality-assurance standards.

Accreditation & rankings

Coordination of national and international accreditation and ranking submissions, including QS and AQACHEI processes.

Policy & documentation

Development, review, and archiving of University policies, procedures, and institutional documents.

Data & reporting

Collection and analysis of institutional data, surveys, and performance indicators to support planning.

Engaging the University community

The Center works with every academic and administrative unit at MEU — supporting self-evaluation, audit, and continuous improvement. Its work is largely behind the scenes, but it is what allows the University to credibly claim the accreditations and rankings prospective students rely on.

From MEU

The Accreditation, Quality, and Information Center at Middle East University is an independent administrative entity. It was founded in 2018/2019 after careful study of the quality and academic-accreditation objectives of many local, Arab, and international academic institutions — in response to the University's keenness to elevate the quality of its academic and administrative performance.

Following MEU's acquisition of the Quality Assurance Certificate (Golden Standard) from the Jordanian Accreditation and Quality Assurance Commission for Higher Education Institutions, the establishment of a dedicated entity became necessary to pursue national and international accreditations and rankings that strengthen the University's academic reputation.
