Overview

The Consulting and Training Center extends MEU's expertise beyond the degree, delivering professional training and consultancy to students, alumni, professionals, and organisations across Jordan.

What the Center offers

Professional & diploma courses

Planning and delivery of training programmes, including diploma-granting courses across scientific and practical fields.

Corporate & community training

Tailored training for companies, public bodies, and community groups, drawing on MEU faculty and facilities.

Consultancy services

Technical and educational studies and advisory services for targeted sectors and organisations.

Continuing education

Upskilling and reskilling pathways for graduates and working professionals seeking to stay current.

Engaging the University community

The Center connects the University to the wider economy, turning academic knowledge into practical skills the labour market needs. It opens MEU's expertise to non-degree learners and contributes to the University's resource development through self-sustaining programmes.

From MEU

Middle East University established the Consulting and Training Center in 2014 to support the University's vision of preparing the leaders of tomorrow and equipping them with the most sophisticated scientific and technical skills.

The Center is responsible for efficiently planning and implementing training programmes at MEU, including diploma-granting courses that cover a wide range of scientific and practical fields.
