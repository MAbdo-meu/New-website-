Welcome to the Faculty

A foundation for excellence in Media

We welcome you on behalf of the faculty and administrative staff. The Faculty of Media is the first faculty in Jordan to award an MA in Media. A subject of study at MEU since its foundation, it was reintroduced as a full faculty in 2019 in recognition of media's role in society — home to the MEU Short Film Festival.

Vision, Mission & Objectives

What we strive for

Vision

To be a leading media faculty in the region, recognised for graduates equipped for modern journalism, digital media and strategic communication.

Mission

Delivering rigorous, practice-oriented education in journalism, digital media and public relations — built on real newsroom practice, production skills and ethical communication.

Objectives

Build production and storytelling capability, deepen ties with regional media organisations, and grow applied research and creative output in digital journalism and strategic communication.
