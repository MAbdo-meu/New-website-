Welcome to the Faculty

A foundation for excellence in Allied Medical Sciences

We welcome you on behalf of the faculty and administrative staff. Allied medical sciences are an ancient profession serving humanity, and our programmes combine rigorous clinical training with a growing research footprint — extending the therapist's role well beyond treatment.

Vision, Mission & Objectives

What we strive for

Vision

To be a leading faculty in allied health education in the region, known for graduates who combine clinical excellence with compassion, and for applied research that improves patient outcomes.

Mission

Delivering hands-on, evidence-based education in physiotherapy, medical laboratory sciences, paramedical & emergency care, and nutrition — built on strong clinical placements and a culture of patient-centred care.

Objectives

Build clinical competence and employability, deepen partnerships with hospitals and health providers, and grow applied research in rehabilitation, diagnostics and public-health nutrition.
