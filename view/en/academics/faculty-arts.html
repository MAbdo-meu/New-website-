Welcome to the Faculty

A foundation for excellence in Arts & Sciences

We welcome you on behalf of the faculty and administrative staff. The Faculty of Arts and Sciences has grown steadily over the years. It comprises four departments — Arabic, English, Political Science and Basic Sciences — providing the foundations that underpin every path at MEU.

Vision, Mission & Objectives

What we strive for

Vision

To be a leading faculty in languages, humanities and foundational sciences in the region, recognised for graduates with strong critical, linguistic and analytical capability.

Mission

Delivering rigorous education in Arabic, English, translation, political science and basic sciences — building the language, thought and scientific literacy that underpin every degree at MEU.

Objectives

Build strong communication and analytical capability, deepen ties with the education, media and public sectors, and grow applied research in linguistics, translation and political studies.
