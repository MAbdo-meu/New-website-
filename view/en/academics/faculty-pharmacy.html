Welcome to the Faculty

A foundation for excellence in Pharmacy

We welcome you on behalf of the faculty and administrative staff. The Faculty of Pharmacy hosts the Department of Pharmacy, responsible for the five-year BSc in Pharmacy that fulfils registration requirements as a pharmacist — strengthened by a joint programme with the University of Strathclyde.

Vision, Mission & Objectives

What we strive for

Vision

To be a leading pharmacy faculty in the region, recognised for graduates equipped for safe, ethical pharmaceutical practice and applied pharmaceutical research.

Mission

Delivering rigorous, practice-oriented education in pharmaceutical sciences and clinical pharmacy — built on strong laboratory training, clinical placements, and international partnership.

Objectives

Build clinical and pharmaceutical competence, deepen ties with hospitals, community pharmacies and manufacturers, and grow applied research in drug development and clinical pharmacy.
