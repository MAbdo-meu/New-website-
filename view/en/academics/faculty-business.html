Vision, Mission & Objectives

What we strive for

Vision

To be a leading business faculty in the region, recognised for developing principled, market-ready graduates and applied research that serves Jordan's economy.

Mission

Delivering rigorous, practice-oriented education in business, finance, accounting and marketing — built on strong academic foundations, industry engagement, and a commitment to ethical leadership.

Objectives

Build employable capability, deepen ties with regional employers, and grow applied research in finance, marketing and organisational behaviour that benefits students and partner organisations alike.
