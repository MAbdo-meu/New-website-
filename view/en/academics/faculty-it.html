Welcome to the Faculty

A foundation for excellence in Information Technology

We welcome you on behalf of the faculty and administrative staff. Founded in September 2005, the Faculty of Information Technology builds human capability and qualifies students for excellence and creativity in a high-quality scientific, research and technical environment, with staff conducting research across networks, cyber security, cloud computing, pervasive computing, IoT and artificial intelligence.

Vision, Mission & Objectives

What we strive for

Vision

Achieving excellence in the field of information technology at the academic and research levels.

Mission

Developing academic programmes that keep abreast of modern technological developments and serve the community.

Objectives

Build student capability, develop staff skills, adopt pioneering community projects and connect with the IT industry.
