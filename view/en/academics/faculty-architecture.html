Welcome to the Faculty

A foundation for excellence in Architecture & Design

We welcome you on behalf of the faculty and administrative staff. Established in 2010/2011, the Faculty of Architecture and Design received its first architecture students in 2011/2012 alongside graphic design cohorts — a studio-led education for the built environment and visual design.

Vision, Mission & Objectives

What we strive for

Vision

To be a leading faculty in architecture and design in the region, recognised for studio-based education that shapes thoughtful, sustainable and creative built and visual environments.

Mission

Delivering rigorous, studio-led education in architecture, interior design and graphic design — built on design fundamentals, technical skill, and engagement with real sites and clients.

Objectives

Build design and technical capability, deepen ties with architecture and design practices, and grow applied research in sustainable design, digital fabrication and visual communication.
