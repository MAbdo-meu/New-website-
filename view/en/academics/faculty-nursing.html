Vision, Mission & Objectives

What we strive for

Vision

To be a leading nursing faculty in the region, recognised for graduates who combine clinical excellence with compassionate, patient-centred care.

Mission

Delivering rigorous, practice-oriented education in nursing — built on strong clinical placements, evidence-based practice, and a commitment to the wellbeing of patients and communities.

Objectives

Build clinical competence and employability, deepen partnerships with hospitals and health providers, and grow applied research in clinical and community-health nursing.
