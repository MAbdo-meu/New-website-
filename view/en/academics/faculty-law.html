Welcome to the Faculty

A foundation for excellence in Law

We welcome you on behalf of the faculty and administrative staff. The Faculty of Law was formed in 2005/2006, first receiving MA students and later establishing a BA programme in Law. It teaches law close to practice — where ministers and the Attorney General come to speak.

Vision, Mission & Objectives

What we strive for

Vision

To be a leading law faculty in the region, recognised for graduates with strong legal reasoning, ethical grounding, and applied research that serves Jordan's legal system.

Mission

Delivering rigorous, practice-oriented education in public and private law — built on strong academic foundations, engagement with the judiciary and bar, and a commitment to justice.

Objectives

Build legal reasoning and advocacy capability, deepen ties with courts, law firms and arbitration centres, and grow applied research in public, private and commercial law.
