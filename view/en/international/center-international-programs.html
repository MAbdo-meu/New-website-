Overview

The International Programs Center is MEU's gateway to the world. It manages inbound and outbound mobility, international partnerships, and the joint and hosted programmes that make MEU one of the most globally connected campuses in Jordan.

What the Center offers

Erasmus+ & credit mobility

Coordination of student and staff exchanges under 40+ Erasmus+ agreements, including application support, credit recognition, and travel logistics.

International partnerships

Development and management of agreements with partner universities for joint programmes, dual awards, and research collaboration.

Incoming international students

A dedicated point of contact for visiting and degree-seeking international students — orientation, support, and integration into campus life.

Global projects & consortia

Participation in EU-funded and multilateral projects that bring international expertise and funding onto campus.

Engaging the University community

The Center internationalises the student experience both on campus and abroad — hosting partner universities at MEU, sending students on exchange, and embedding a global perspective across faculties. It works closely with admissions, faculties, and student services to ensure every student can access an international opportunity.

From MEU

MEU is a beacon of international collaboration — bridging borders and building futures through unique collaboration propositions that open a world of opportunities for students and create impact beyond the walls of the campus.

MEU's international collaboration follows a simple strategy for engagement with the world: to internationalise not only the opportunities available to its students and faculty, but to bring the world to its campus — standing as the only true international campus in Jordan, a university that proudly hosts other universities on its grounds. Students gain international exposure both on campus and through the mobility opportunities provided to them, empowering them to pursue their dreams wherever in the world that may lead.
