UK Degrees at MEU

The first university in Jordan to host international programmes on campus — in partnership with the University of Bedfordshire and the University of Strathclyde.

At Middle East University we host quality programmes from renowned international universities. Our commitment to globalising higher education has led to strategic partnerships with two prestigious UK institutions, bringing UK undergraduate, postgraduate and PhD degrees to Amman.

Five years on, UK Degrees MEU has built a legacy of graduates making their mark worldwide.
