One-time charges

Enrollment application and the refundable deposit are paid once at the start of your studies. The deposit is returned on graduation, subject to clearance.

Per-semester charges

Registration fees apply each regular semester. Tuition per credit hour is added on top, billed at course registration.

Optional add-ons

Health insurance and transportation are optional services. Non-Jordanian students pay a small service fee each semester.
