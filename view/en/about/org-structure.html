Middle East University operates as a private university under the Jordanian Universities Law No. 18 of 2018, which grants private universities their own organisational structure across administrative, technical and financial divisions. Authority flows from the Board of Trustees through the President, supported by councils, deanships, academic faculties and service departments.

Statutory basis

Jordanian Universities Law No. 18 of 2018

Article 6/B/2 — A private university has its own organisational structure that runs its administrative, technical and financial divisions, directly supervised by the President.

Article 12/B — The President may delegate any of their powers to deputies, deans and directors according to their fields of specialisation, provided the delegation is specific and in written form.

Article 28/A — The Board is directly responsible for spending, capital construction projects, buildings, amenities and necessary maintenance for the private university, and manages funding per the approved plan by the Board of Trustees.
