Vision

A leading and influential university locally and regionally.

Mission

Middle East University seeks to prepare a graduate who keeps pace with the requirements of the local and international labour market, to strengthen distinctive applied and impactful scientific research, to serve the community, and to strengthen international academic partnerships.

University objectives

  • Adopting governance and effective leadership in University management
  • Preparing and qualifying human competencies
  • Developing institutional administrative performance
  • Developing and updating academic plans and programmes
  • Professionalising study plans
  • Strengthening academic partnerships and hosted programmes
  • Developing and strengthening technical education
  • Creating a stimulating learning environment that attracts innovation, invention, creativity and entrepreneurship
  • Raising graduates' readiness and their life, professional and digital skills for the labour market
  • Improving the student's university life and experience
  • Enhancing the quality of educational services
  • Developing the campus digital infrastructure
  • Integrating artificial intelligence applications in the academic and administrative environments
  • Creating a research environment that stimulates innovation in research output
  • Providing applicable scientific solutions to national priorities
  • Establishing research centres of excellence
  • Building effective partnerships with civil society institutions
  • Promoting volunteer work and active good citizenship
  • Supporting the achievement of the sustainable development goals
  • Political development through raising awareness and participation
  • Consolidating the University's identity
  • Improving the University's position in global rankings
  • Obtaining local and international specialised academic accreditations
  • Diversifying the sources of financial resources

Core values

Seriousness, commitment and learning: Seriousness, commitment and the pursuit of learning are among the most important references of the University's value system.

Openness to academic spaces: Openness to local, regional and global academic spaces is an indispensable necessity for exchanging information and establishing scientific and cultural partnerships in the service of all humanity.

Respect for the community's capacities: Respect for the values of society and its historical and national heritage is one of the most important pillars of the University's value system.

Dialogue and nourishing minds: Providing a suitable atmosphere for dialogue, exchange of ideas and nourishing the minds of all groups at the University — faculty, administrators and students — is an important value for rejecting violence and intolerance and embodying organisational culture, good taste and the rules of etiquette and protocol.

Respect and recognition of achievement: Treating faculty and administrative staff with respect, valuing academic degrees and safeguarding their prestige, respecting students, their families and all stakeholders, and recognising outstanding achievements are among the highest values, because they realise the concept of the respected university.

Noble morals: A value relied upon in the University's dealings with all parties, institutions and individuals.

Credibility and optimism: Two values that influence the building of a refined and intimate university community, characterised by hope for the future, tolerance in life and flexibility in thinking.

Job security and safeguarding freedom of expression: Two fundamental values in organising the relationship of rights and mutual duties between the University and its members.

Institutional belonging and loyalty: Deep belief in the University's mission and vision and strengthening the spirit of individual and collective responsibility towards achieving its goals through sincerity in work is one of the University's most distinctive values.
