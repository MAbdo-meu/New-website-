Message from the President

Welcome to Middle East University. It is an honour to lead such a young, but outstanding institution of higher learning. We are home to distinguished faculty, administrators, staff, and — of course — students, all with a high degree of commitment to academic and research excellence.

Middle East University has succeeded in remapping its mission along international lines by offering the first hosted and joint academic programmes in Jordan. We believe that outstanding teaching, innovative research, and community outreach go hand in hand — clearly reflected in our mission, vision and long-term objectives.

As we take MEU into the future, we remain committed to a student-focused model geared towards preparing leaders. Our programme objectives delineate immediate and future student outcomes, taking into account the importance of sustainable proficiencies and entrepreneurial skills.

Once again, welcome to the MEU website — the gate through which we present our vibrant and dynamic vision for a brighter future.

Prof. Salam Al-MahadinPresident, Middle East University
