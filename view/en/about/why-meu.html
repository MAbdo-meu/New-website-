A university built for outcomes

MEU combines national accreditation, UK-partnered degrees, and a campus minutes from Queen Alia International Airport. Students choose MEU for its focus on employability, its modern facilities, and its standing among Jordan's private universities.

What sets MEU apart

  • Accredited programmes with AQACHEI Golden Level standing
  • UK degree programmes delivered on the Amman campus
  • Strong graduate-employment focus and labour-market preparation
  • A SMART University digital infrastructure
  • Active research culture and scholarships
  • A supportive, activity-rich campus environment
