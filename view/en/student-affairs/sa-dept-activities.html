Student Activities Department

The Department runs three divisions:

Sports Activity Division

  • Organising internal and external tournaments across a range of sports.
  • Delivering diverse activities aligned with students' needs and interests.
  • Opening participation to the widest possible number of students and staff.
  • Instilling sound concepts of physical education and sports awareness.
  • Raising team performance through indoor and outdoor courts, the beach court, and games halls.
  • Establishing sports camps and making good use of students' free time.

Arts Activity Division

  • Giving students opportunities to practise and refine their artistic hobbies and creativity.
  • Sponsoring the gifted in poetry, music, and theatre.
  • Establishing artistic troupes and participating in art festivals and competitions.
  • Running courses in singing, musical instruments, Debkeh (folk dance), drawing, and theatre.

Cultural & Social Activity Division

  • Raising awareness and refining the university student's personality.
  • Promoting public and community service through workshops, seminars, and voluntary initiatives.
  • Supervising cultural and social activities and motivating student participation.
  • Taking part in events that serve the local community.
