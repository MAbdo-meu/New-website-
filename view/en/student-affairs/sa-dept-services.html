Student Services & International Students

Student Services

  • Monitoring the quality of services provided to students.
  • Supervising student societies and clubs and overseeing the election of their bodies.
  • Issuing certificates of good conduct in line with University instructions.
  • Covering absences related to student activities.
  • Following up and supervising student trips per established procedures.
  • Overseeing scholarship and loan applications and contributing to the Student Fund's management.
  • Receiving and advertising lost items for return to their owners.
  • Contributing to the reception of new students and to the graduation ceremony.
  • Maintaining continuous communication with students.

International Students Division

  • Maintaining continuous communication with international students.
  • Activating the role of international students within the University and Jordanian society.
  • Helping international students resolve issues within University regulations.
  • Organising regular meetings and dialogues between international students and University officials.
  • Arranging meetings with the Cultural Attachés of students' countries.
  • Supporting exhibitions and celebrations on students' national occasions.
