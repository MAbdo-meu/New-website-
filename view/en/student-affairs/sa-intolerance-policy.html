Protection Against Intolerance & Discrimination Policy

Purpose. To foster a secure and equitable university environment, free from all forms of violence and discrimination.

Objectives

To prevent all forms of violence and discrimination within Middle East University; to cultivate a professional environment devoid of hostility and prejudice; and to define the mechanisms and channels available for reporting incidents of discrimination and violence.

Definitions

University Members
Faculty members, administrative staff, and students.
Discrimination
Any direct or indirect conduct involving differentiation, exclusion, isolation, or preference based on political opinion, social origin, or physical appearance, which impedes equal opportunity or treatment in employment and occupation. Differentiation based on the inherent requirements of a specific role does not constitute discrimination.
Violence
Any aggressive act likely to result in physical, psychological, sexual, legal, economic, or social harm.

Institutional Procedures

To achieve its objectives, the University has adopted the following measures:

Core Values

Ethical integrity, dialogue and intellectual enrichment, and job security with freedom of expression — actively promoted among all members.

Admissions & Recruitment

Students admitted on merit per Ministry policies; faculty and administrative applicants selected strictly on qualifications and competence.

Reporting Mechanisms

Any student, staff member, or visitor may file a formal complaint via the official "Suggestions, Complaints, and Appreciation" procedures, reviewed weekly.

Anonymity & Grievances

Complainants may remain anonymous, whether reporting on paper or through the electronic portal; the focus is on resolving root causes, not identifying the claimant.

Disciplinary Action

Violations are investigated through relevant councils — students under the Student Disciplinary Policy, faculty and staff under the Accountability Policy.

Orientation & Awareness

New appointees are briefed on the Code of Conduct governing professional and interpersonal relations on campus.

Prohibition of Harassment

All academic and administrative staff are prohibited from engaging in or inciting any abuse against members or visitors; sanctions apply to breaches.

Equality & Non-Discrimination

Faculty must uphold students' rights with impartiality and respect; discrimination on race, gender, belief, social status, or any protected characteristic is forbidden.

Support & Inclusion

Adequate care for students with special educational needs, backed by the Support Policy for Students with Disabilities to ensure full integration.

Digital Moderation

Administrative units monitor the University's official social media to prevent and remove derogatory or offensive content.

Behavioural Awareness

Educational workshops and group guidance sessions on etiquette, mutual respect, acceptance of diversity, and the eradication of bullying.

Quality Management Review

Senior Management reviews complaint volumes and resolution rates annually within the Management Review Meeting, aligned to ISO 9001:2015.

Whistleblower Protection

Individuals reporting incidents in good faith are protected from retaliation or adverse effects on their academic or professional progression.

Security & Access

Comprehensive CCTV across buildings and common areas; campus access restricted to authorised personnel via valid University identification.

Mutual Respect

Defamation of religious beliefs or political opinions — on or off campus — is prohibited, with respect mandated for all members' rights.

Student Engagement

Student clubs bring together varied cultural and intellectual backgrounds, promoting tolerance and rejecting intolerance.

Judicial & Investigative Councils

The University has established independent bodies to adjudicate violations:

Primary Disciplinary Council for Faculty Members Appellate Disciplinary Council for Faculty Members Disciplinary Council for Administrative Staff Student Disciplinary Council Faculty Investigative Committees

Governance, Review & KPIs

Review Cycle

Biennially (every two years), or as required based on feedback from the university community.

Responsibility

Adherence to and implementation of this policy rests with all University Members.

KPI — Resolution Rate

The percentage of submitted grievances successfully addressed and closed.

KPI — Satisfaction Indices

Results from stakeholder surveys on the university environment and institutional climate.
