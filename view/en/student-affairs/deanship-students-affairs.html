About the Deanship

The Deanship of Student Affairs supports the educational process at Middle East University through its direct communication with students, making it the University's tower of strength. It dedicates its efforts and resources to serving students throughout their academic journeys, with the aim of bringing forward positive, well-informed leaders.

Students are the focal point of an integral educational process. The Deanship therefore seeks their intellectual preparation, enabling them to express their opinions within moderate, purposeful dialogue. It attends to the social, psychological, and physical dimensions of student life, and develops students' talents and capabilities by providing a suitable university environment.

The Deanship gives students the opportunity and encouragement to take part in the wide range of extracurricular activities it organises within structured programmes — activities designed to prepare distinct alumni who contribute to the advancement of their societies towards a bright future.
