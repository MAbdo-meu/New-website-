Erasmus+ International Credit Mobility

Erasmus+ is an EU-funded programme supporting the development and modernisation of higher education in partner countries. MEU has established more than 40 partnership agreements with distinguished universities across Europe under the Erasmus+ International Credit Mobility (ICM) and Consortium schemes.
