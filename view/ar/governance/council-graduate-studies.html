Role

The Council of Graduate Studies sets the rules and regulations of MEU's graduate programmes and advises faculties and departments on graduate matters.

Membership

The Council includes the Dean of Graduate Studies and Scientific Research and the deans of the faculties, alongside representatives of the local community.
