كلمة الرئيس

Welcome to Middle East University. It is an honour to lead such a young but outstanding institution. We are home to distinguished faculty, administrators, staff and students with a high degree of commitment to academic and research excellence. MEU has remapped its mission along international lines by offering the first hosted and joint academic programmes in Jordan. We believe outstanding teaching, innovative research and community outreach go hand in hand — and we remain committed to a student-focused model geared towards preparing leaders.
