About the Unit

The Control and Internal Audit Unit reports independently to the Board of Trustees. It monitors compliance with the University's regulations and with the instructions of the Ministry of Higher Education and the Accreditation and Quality Assurance Commission, and reviews financial and administrative controls across the University.

Scope of work

  • Internal audit of financial transactions, procurement and payroll.
  • Review of administrative and academic compliance with approved regulations.
  • Risk assessment and follow-up on corrective actions.
  • Reporting findings and recommendations to the Board of Trustees and the President.
