نظرة عامة محتوى مبدئي

House of Art (بيت الفن) is envisaged as MEU's home for the visual and performing arts — a studio, gallery, and rehearsal space where students from every faculty can make, exhibit, and perform work alongside practising artists.

What the House of Art would offer محتوى مبدئي

Studios & workshops

Open studio space for painting, drawing, ceramics, and craft, with scheduled workshops led by visiting artists.

Exhibition gallery

A rotating gallery for student, staff, and community exhibitions throughout the academic year.

Music & performance

Rehearsal rooms and a small performance space for music, theatre, and spoken-word evenings.

Community programmes

Short courses and school outreach that open MEU's arts facilities to the wider Amman community.
