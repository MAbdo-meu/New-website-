نظرة عامة

The Innovation & Entrepreneurship Center is where MEU students turn ideas into ventures. It functions as the University's startup engine — a place to test a concept, find a co-founder, build a prototype, and pitch to real investors.

ما يقدّمه المركز

Idea-to-venture incubation

Structured incubation tracks that take students from a raw idea through validation, prototyping, and a market-ready business model, with workspace and mentorship at each stage.

Mentorship & investor network

One-to-one guidance from entrepreneurs and industry mentors, plus curated introductions to angel investors, accelerators, and funding bodies across the region.

Competitions & global exposure

Preparation and sponsorship for national and international entrepreneurship competitions, hackathons, and demo days.

Workshops & bootcamps

Hands-on sessions in business modelling, financial planning, pitching, intellectual property, and go-to-market strategy.

إشراك مجتمع الجامعة

The Center is open to students from every faculty, pairing technical talent with business and design students on mixed founding teams. It runs open-call competitions, collaborates with the local startup ecosystem, and connects graduates to the labour market as founders and employers rather than only job-seekers.

من الجامعة

The Innovation and Entrepreneurship Center (IEC) at MEU provides students with the help they need to learn how to convert their innovative conceptual ideas into actual businesses and fully realised projects.

Thanks to the IEC's focus on internationalism and its global perspective, students who work with the IEC learn the skills they need to successfully network with investors, turn their ideas into actionable and profitable projects, and compete on a global scale. The IEC also helps MEU students participate in international entrepreneurship competitions.
