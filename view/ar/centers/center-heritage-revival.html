نظرة عامة

The Yaqoub Nasir al-Din Heritage Revival Center documents, preserves, and revives Jordanian and Arab cultural, environmental, and historical heritage — serving researchers, students, and the wider community.

ما يقدّمه المركز

Documentation & archiving

Collection and preservation of documents, manuscripts, and records of archaeological and heritage sites.

Research support

Access to source materials and manuscripts for students and researchers pursuing heritage-related scholarship.

Awareness & outreach

Programmes and events that build public awareness of the value of Jordanian and Arab heritage.

Heritage & environment

Initiatives linking cultural heritage to environmental awareness and to the tourism sector.

إشراك مجتمع الجامعة

The Center anchors MEU's cultural identity, giving students and faculty a living resource for heritage research and connecting the University to national preservation efforts and the communities whose heritage it documents.

من الجامعة

The Yaqoub Nasir al-Din Heritage Revival Center works to highlight the identity of cultural, environmental, and historical heritage elements in Jordanian and Arab society, and to revive that heritage for researchers, students, and the wider community.
