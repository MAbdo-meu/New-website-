مركز الإرشاد والخدمات النفسية

General objective: to provide psychological and social support to students and university affiliates — promoting mental health, helping students adapt to the university environment, and achieving psychological wellbeing.

Services provided

  • Individual & group counselling — assisting students facing psychological or social difficulties, and addressing anxiety, depression, tension, low self-confidence, and related concerns.
  • Psychological awareness — group guidance workshops on life skills such as emotional intelligence, effective communication, and stress management, to support psychological equilibrium.
  • Case follow-up — ensuring the continuity of psychological support over time.
  • Community collaboration — partnering with local institutions to promote a culture of counselling, social integration, and student awareness, alongside activities that foster wellbeing.
