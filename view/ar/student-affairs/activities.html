Student life

The Deanship cultivates student skills across cultural, recreational, athletic and social domains, organising events and activities that foster engagement within the University community.

Skills for the labour market

Beyond activities, the Deanship runs courses that prepare students for the labour market and keeps in touch with graduates, highlighting their success stories in line with MEU's vision to prepare leaders.
