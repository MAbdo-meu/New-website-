Welcome

The Deanship of Student Affairs supports the educational process at MEU through direct communication with students. It dedicates its resources to students throughout their academic journey, helping bring forward positive, well-informed leaders.

The Deanship focuses on the social, psychological and physical sides of student life, developing talents and capabilities by providing a supportive university environment and a wide range of extracurricular activities.
