كلمة العميد

Dr. Hazem Nsourعميد شؤون الطلبة بالوكالة

The Deanship of Student Affairs constitutes a fundamental pillar of Middle East University — a position it has assumed through its close and direct relationship with students, and through its commitment to their responsibilities and priorities.

In this capacity, the Deanship endeavours to cultivate students' skills across a diverse range of cultural, recreational, athletic, and social domains. To this end, it organises a variety of activities and events designed to foster student engagement within the university community.

Alongside enhancing students' abilities and developing their talents through courses that prepare them for the labour market, the Deanship maintains communication with the University's graduates and highlights their success stories — in line with Middle East University's vision to "Prepare Leaders."

The Deanship extends its wishes for the success of our students in their educational journey on the campus of Middle East University — a university distinguished by its environment, plans, and study programmes.
