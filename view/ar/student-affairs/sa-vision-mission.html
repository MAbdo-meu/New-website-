الرؤية

To be a leading deanship that nurtures well-rounded, responsible student leaders — intellectually engaged, socially aware, and ready to contribute to their communities.

الرسالة

To support the educational process through direct communication with students; to develop their talents and skills across cultural, recreational, athletic, and social domains; to safeguard their social, psychological, and physical wellbeing; and to prepare distinguished graduates who serve and advance their societies.

الأهداف

  • Prepare students intellectually and enable them to express their views within moderate, purposeful dialogue.
  • Attend to students' social, psychological, and physical needs throughout their academic journey.
  • Develop students' talents and capabilities by providing a suitable university environment.
  • Encourage participation in structured extracurricular activities and programmes.
  • Equip students with skills for the labour market through targeted courses and training.
  • Maintain communication with alumni and highlight their success stories.
  • Foster active citizenship, volunteerism, and community service.
