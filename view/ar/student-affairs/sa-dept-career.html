Career Guidance & Alumni Follow-up Office

Established under the King Abdullah II Fund for Development, the Office grew out of recommendations of the Jordan Youth Forum held under the patronage of His Majesty King Abdullah II. On 24/12/2014 the Higher Education Accreditation Institutions Council required universities in Jordan to establish such an office within the Deanship of Student Affairs. Middle East University has supported the Office since its inception, providing the means to strengthen communication between alumni and the labour market.

الأهداف

The primary objective is to help provide a suitable university environment in which students gain the experience and skills that increase their employability. Subsidiary objectives include:

  • Holding career-guidance sessions to help students understand labour-market needs, saturated specialisations, and career-path requirements.
  • Running training courses for students and alumni to build their capacities and skills.
  • Fostering active citizenship through participation in voluntary programmes and projects.
  • Strengthening partnerships between the public and private sectors, NGOs, international organisations, and the University.

What career guidance means

Career guidance is the process of advising and assisting individuals in making sound professional decisions, understanding their skills and interests, identifying suitable options, and building a clear action plan — grounded in honest self-assessment of personal and professional capabilities.
