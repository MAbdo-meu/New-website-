نظرة عامة

Founded at the start of 2018, the Deanship of Graduate Studies and Scientific Research oversees all post-graduate studies and scientific research at MEU. On the graduate side it supervises admissions, monitors academic and research performance, and fosters an environment that helps students reach higher levels of achievement.
