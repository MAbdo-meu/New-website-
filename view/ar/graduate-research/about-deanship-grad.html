Purpose

The Deanship facilitates a graduate experience that instils the desire to pursue advanced study. It is dedicated to graduate students' future success and supports academic research as a route to the development of society.

Aims

The Deanship organises training courses, workshops, conferences and seminars on post-graduate study, providing a platform for exchanging expertise with local and international institutions.
