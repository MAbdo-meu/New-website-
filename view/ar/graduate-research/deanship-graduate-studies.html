عن العمادة

Founded at the beginning of 2018, the Deanship of Graduate Studies and Scientific Research oversees all post-graduate studies and scientific research affairs at MEU. It supervises graduate-programme admissions and monitors graduate students' academic and research performance, fostering an educational environment built around students' needs so they can reach more sophisticated levels of academic and research achievement.

The Deanship works to keep pace with accreditation and quality-assurance standards in service of the University's aim for sustainable development.

It encourages MEU's faculty and researchers to undertake original, innovative research projects that enrich knowledge and serve the community, and is always eager to cooperate with academic and research institutions through joint research and the exchange of expertise.
