كلمة العميد

Prof. Ahmad Musaعميد الدراسات العليا والبحث العلمي

The Deanship of Graduate Studies and Scientific Research was founded to support the University as it works towards achieving its vision and mission. It is dedicated to fulfilling the community's needs and providing the local and international labour market with qualified professionals.

The Deanship's main objective is to facilitate a rewarding graduate experience that instils in scholars the desire to pursue advanced degrees, and to cultivate a culture of rigorous, applied research across the University.
