Apply for a talent scholarship

Talent awards recognise students who represent MEU in sport, the arts, media production and public speaking. Applications open with each intake and are assessed by a panel from the relevant faculty and the Deanship of Student Affairs.

  1. Choose and apply to your programme first.
  2. Submit the talent application with a portfolio, reel or competition record.
  3. Attend a trial, audition or interview on campus.
  4. Receive the award decision in writing before fee settlement.
