Tuition Fees & Insurances

A clear, published schedule of tuition, registration, insurance and service charges — for both regular and summer semesters.

Admissions

Fee structure

All amounts are shown in Jordanian Dinar (JD) and US Dollar (US$). Some items are one-time charges; others are billed per semester. Optional items are clearly marked.

Higher Diploma — Tuition, Insurance & Other Charges

The table below reflects MEU’s published fee schedule for Higher Diploma studies. Tuition per credit hour is assessed at the point of course registration and is not included in the items below.

Information Table Regular Semester (JD) Regular Semester (US$) Summer Semester (JD) Summer Semester (US$)

Figures shown for reference; the Finance Department’s issued invoice is the binding statement of fees due.

One-time charges

Enrollment application and the refundable deposit are paid once at the start of your studies. The deposit is returned on graduation, subject to clearance.

Per-semester charges

Registration fees apply each regular semester. Tuition per credit hour is added on top, billed at course registration.

Optional add-ons

Health insurance and transportation are optional services. Non-Jordanian students pay a small service fee each semester.
